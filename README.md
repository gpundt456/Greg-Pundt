My development philosophy is **local-first automation with minimal recurring costs**.

I prefer self-hosted software and locally running AI models over paid inference APIs whenever practical. My goal is to build reusable systems capable of operating continuously without per-request API charges or third-party usage limits.

Most of my projects are developed and tested on an **NVIDIA RTX 3080 (10 GB VRAM) with 32 GB RAM**, making hardware efficiency and resource management important parts of my work.
